# Intake Q&A (ask before building; question tool, ≤ 4 per call, recommended option first)

Ask what the user can't be guessed from the brief. Skip any question the brief already answers, and say which default you took.

## Round 1: the story
1. **What object carries the story?** PO (recommended for distributors/suppliers) · invoice · new lead/form · support ticket · other
2. **What is the pain, in numbers?** Use the hourly rate × hours a day × 260 workdays. Default $20/hr × 5 h = **$26,000 a year**. Ask whether they want their own figures.
3. **Who does the manual work on screen?** A first name (default "Maria") and a role.
4. **Where does it get retyped?** Pick 2 real tools plus 2 generics (default QuickBooks ERP + HubSpot CRM + customer folder + email). Logos are shown only if they are true for the prospect or the integration.

## Round 2: the voice and the sender
5. **Opener:** "Hey, this is random… but I peeped something in your business that could be interesting." (recommended) · a more formal opener · a custom one
6. **Who is the iMessage from?** The sender's name and headshot path. Default: the user and their branding headshot.
7. **Voice:** Brian (warm, casual US male, recommended) · another ElevenLabs voice ID · the user's own cloned voice
8. **CTA:** "Worth a look? Just reply." (recommended) · "Book 15 minutes" · custom

## Round 3: the delivery
9. **Formats:** 9:16 + 16:9 (recommended) · 9:16 only
10. **Music:** a bed under the voice plus a Voice+SFX twin (recommended) · no music at all
11. **The exception in the demo:** the one thing the system catches. Default: a quantity 10× the customer's usual, with "Use 120 / Keep 1,200".
12. **Brand end card:** the wordmark file, shown ALONE at the end.
